# Conversion improvements

Added a distinct call-request path alongside the existing iMe demonstration form on training heroes, the mobile menu and every page footer. The contact page explains the conversation, suggests including suitable times and a time zone, and keeps phone and email visible.

No scheduler URL has been supplied. Call requests remain email handoffs; they do not automatically capture leads, send messages or reserve appointments. No conversion uplift is claimed without measurement. Company policy, required fields and product enquiry/privacy destinations are unchanged.

Validation: TypeScript, lint, build, three enquiry tests and 78 HTTP/source checks passed. Browser navigation from the homepage call CTA to the updated contact page passed. Actual email delivery and calendar booking were not tested.
